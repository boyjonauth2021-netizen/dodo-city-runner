CREDITS

Dodo model — “Dodo [ Extinct Bird ]” by BlueMesh on Sketchfab, licensed CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0). Rigged model used as supplied; run, jump and slide poses are animated procedurally in-game and the broken exported eyes were replaced with simple ones.

Characters & animals — Quaternius (CC0): chef, suit man, man, three women, adventurer, farmer, worker, shiba, chicken, cow, pig, deer, sheep, cars and traffic cone. “Male Officer” by J-Toastie (poly.pizza, CC BY 3.0) is the Policier.

Props — “Cartoony Purple Motorcycle” by AliceCassie (CC0), “Fruit Crate” by BlenderVoyage (CC0), Quaternius boats, stall and rocks (CC0). Everything else (carts, trucks, tractor, scenery, sky) is generated in code.

Music — instrumental sega / seggae supplied with the project (looping, one track per level). Tousse Sali and Ratintin (bundled tracks, artist unknown); Roul Matak — Bigg Frankii ft. Jor'Dan; Sega Tioula — Bigg Frankii ft. Baron & DJ DarkSoul; Rêve Nou Ancêtres — Cassiya; Li Tourner — Dj Assad ft. Alain Ramanisum & Willy William; Angui La Kordé — Médérice & Clarel Armel; La Métisse — Zulu ft. Mario Ramsamy; Posson Salé — Sylvio Louise; Tik Tiké — Laura Beg; plus Natcho Beti (instrumental) — artist unknown. These are instrumental versions of commercial songs — confirm rights before any public release (see README).

Engine — three.js (MIT). Buses, metro trains, buildings and sounds are generated in code.

Maps — Level layouts follow ordered polylines of real named roads stitched from OpenStreetMap data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). See maps/README.md.

Mountains & sea — Recognisable Mauritian silhouettes (Le Pouce, Pieter Both, Le Morne, Lion Mountain, Trois Mamelles, and others) are hand-profiled from published elevations and reference views, placed on the correct bearing from each route. The lagoon water is a procedural shader (waves, fresnel, sun glint, reef surf). Boats are generated in-engine (CC0).
